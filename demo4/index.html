Roadside Hunt

A VR scavenger hunt set along a desert roadside, combining exploration, environmental interaction, and configurable comfort options.

View project on GitHub ↗Back to all work

About the project

Players search the environment for collectible coins, manipulate roadside props, and use their findings with an interactive vending machine. Moving traffic adds risk and a reason to stay aware of the surroundings.

The experience supports smooth movement or teleporting, snap or smooth turning, seated and standing modes, vignette settings, and adjustable movement speed.

Highlights

  • ObjectiveFind and use collectible coins
