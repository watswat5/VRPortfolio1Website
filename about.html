About me

From circuits to software.

I’m Chris Antepenko, a software and embedded systems developer studying computer science at the University of Georgia.

My background

My experience spans microcontroller firmware, robotics testing, electronics prototyping, and software development. I’ve worked on battery monitoring systems, connected hardware, and consumer electronics, bringing code and physical systems together.

Alongside my computer science studies, I build projects that let me explore how systems work—from a CHIP-8 emulator to a portable synthesizer. My Unity portfolios extend that interest into interactive 3D experiences.
